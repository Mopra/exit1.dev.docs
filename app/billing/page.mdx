# Plans & Pricing

exit1.dev offers four plans. Pick the one that fits your monitoring needs. You can upgrade or downgrade anytime.

To upgrade, change your card, download invoices or cancel, see [Manage your plan and payment](/billing/manage).

The ladder is **monotonic**: every tier is a strict superset of the one below it. Caps only rise, check intervals only get faster, and no feature is ever taken away by moving up. Check interval is the primary axis (*how fast do you want to know*), with the team-and-scale features layered on at Nano and Pro.

Annual billing saves 17 to 25% depending on the plan, about 20% on average. Prices are in US dollars, and any tax is added at checkout.

Every paid plan starts with a **7-day free trial**. You pick a plan, the trial
runs for 7 days, and billing starts after that unless you cancel first. The trial
is available once per account.

> **API and MCP access are on every plan, including Free.** They are the same
> entitlement: if a plan can mint an API key, it can use the MCP server. Free
> mints one. See [MCP integration](/integrations/mcp).

> **The Live page is on every plan too.** Real-time WebSocket probe streaming
> with scrolling charts and raw-probe tables is not a paid feature.

## Free

Genuinely usable monitoring with no expiry and no credit card. This is the plan
that never runs out, as opposed to the 7-day trial that comes with the paid ones.

- 50 monitors, 5-minute intervals
- Email alerts (50/hr, 300/mo)
- 1 webhook integration
- 1 API key with MCP access
- 1 public status page
- SSL certificate monitoring
- Live probe stream
- 60 days of history
- Bulk import and bulk edit

## Indie: $4/mo ($3/mo annual)

For sites you want watched closely, with your own name on the status page.

- 100 monitors, 1-minute intervals
- Email alerts (100/hr, 800/mo)
- 3 webhook integrations
- 3 API keys with MCP access
- 1 status page with the full drag-and-drop builder, with your logo, colours and layout
- DNS record monitoring (5-minute minimum interval)
- 90 days of history

## Nano: $9/mo ($7/mo annual)

Production monitoring for small teams.

- 250 monitors, 30-second intervals
- Email alerts (250/hr, 2,500/mo)

- 10 API keys with MCP access
- 5 status pages with the builder

- Maintenance mode
- Region choice: pin checks to US, EU, or Asia
- Stats and timeline views
- 365 days of history

Everything exit1 does, at the highest limits.

## Retired plans

**Agency** was retired and folded into Pro, which carries every former Agency
entitlement. Existing Agency subscribers keep working with no capability loss.
**Scale** and **Starter** are legacy plan keys that resolve to Pro and Nano
respectively.

## Upgrading

1. Click **Plans & billing** in the left sidebar, or go straight to
   [app.exit1.dev/billing?tab=plans](https://app.exit1.dev/billing?tab=plans)
2. On the **Plans** tab, pick a cadence with the Monthly / Annual toggle
3. Click **Get Indie / Nano / Pro** on the plan you want (if you already pay, it
   says **Upgrade to Indie / Nano / Pro**)
4. Confirm at checkout. Your account is upgraded immediately

If you already pay for a plan, checkout credits the unused remainder of your
current billing period against the new plan, so you only pay the difference.

Step-by-step help for changing your card, getting receipts with your company
details, cancelling and failed payments is on
[Manage your plan and payment](/billing/manage).

## Switching between monthly and annual

On the **Plans** tab, flip the Monthly / Annual toggle. Your current plan's card
changes its button to **Switch to annual** (or **Switch to monthly**). Annual
saves 17 to 25% depending on the plan, about 20% on average.

## Downgrading

1. Open the **Plans** tab in **Billing**
2. Click **Downgrade to** the plan you want
3. Read the summary of what changes, then confirm
4. Your plan changes at the end of the current billing period
5. Resources above the new tier's limits are **paused, never deleted**

On a downgrade, exit1 prunes to the new plan's caps rather than switching
everything off: the newest monitors within your new cap keep running, checks
beyond it are disabled (not deleted), check intervals are widened to the new
floor, and extra webhooks, API keys and status pages are disabled. Status-page
branding and pinned regions are cleared if the new plan doesn't include them.
Everything comes back if you re-subscribe.

## Cancelling

Cancelling drops you to Free rather than to a cheaper paid plan. On the
**Plans** tab, click **Cancel subscription** on the Free card, or use **Cancel
subscription** on the Billing overview. You keep paid features until the end of
the billing period. The same pruning rules as a downgrade apply once you land
on Free. See [Manage your plan and payment](/billing/manage#cancel) for the
full steps.
