# Manage your plan and payment

Everything about paying for exit1.dev happens on one page. This guide shows you where it is and which buttons to click.

## Where to find billing

Click **Plans & billing** in the left sidebar, near the bottom just above Docs.
You can also click your plan name in the top bar, or **Billing** in the account menu under your avatar.
Or go straight to [app.exit1.dev/billing](https://app.exit1.dev/billing).

The page has these tabs:

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Your current plan and quick buttons |
| **Plans** | Compare plans, upgrade, downgrade, switch monthly or annual |
| **Payment** | Your saved cards |
| **Invoices** | Every charge, with a downloadable receipt |
| **Org** | Company name, address and VAT number for your receipts |

If you have never paid, you only see the **Plans** tab. The other tabs appear after your first payment.

## Upgrade

1. Open [app.exit1.dev/billing?tab=plans](https://app.exit1.dev/billing?tab=plans).
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Annual** with the toggle at the top.
3. On the plan you want, click **Get Indie**, **Get Nano** or **Get Pro**. If you can get a free trial, the button mentions the trial instead. If you already pay, the button says **Upgrade to Indie**, **Upgrade to Nano** or **Upgrade to Pro**.
4. A checkout panel slides in. Enter your card and confirm.
5. Your new plan starts right away.

If you have never paid before, you get a **7-day free trial** first. You are not charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel before then. The trial is available once per account.

If you already pay for a plan, you only pay the difference for the rest of your current billing period.

Prices are in US dollars. Any tax is added at checkout.

| Plan | Monthly | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 |
| Indie | $4/mo | $36/yr ($3/mo) |
| Nano | $9/mo | $84/yr ($7/mo) |
| Pro | $24/mo | $240/yr ($20/mo) |

## Switch between monthly and annual

Annual billing saves 17 to 25% depending on the plan, about 20% on average.

1. Open the **Plans** tab.
2. Flip the toggle to **Annual** (or **Monthly**).
3. On your current plan, click **Switch to annual** (or **Switch to monthly**).
4. Confirm.

## Change your card

1. Open the **Payment** tab and click **Add or change card**. On the **Overview** tab, the **Update card** button does the same thing.
2. A secure billing window opens.
3. Add your new card.
4. Set the new card as your default.
5. Remove the old card if you no longer want it saved.

Your next charge goes to your default card.

## Download invoices and receipts

1. Open the **Invoices** tab. On the **Overview** tab you can also click **Invoices**.
2. Find the charge you need. Every charge is listed.
3. Click **Receipt** to download it as a PDF.

To see your monthly statements, click **Monthly statements**.

### Put your company name and VAT number on receipts

1. Open the **Org** tab.
2. Fill in your company name, legal name, address and VAT or tax ID. You can also add custom fields, such as a purchase order number.
3. Save.

These details are printed on your receipts.

## Cancel

1. Click **Cancel subscription**. You find it on the **Overview** tab, and on the Free card in the **Plans** tab.
2. Confirm that you want to cancel.
3. A subscription window opens. Confirm the cancellation there too.

What happens next:

- You keep your paid plan until the end of the period you already paid for. Then your account moves to Free.
- Monitors above the Free limit are switched off. They are **never deleted**.
- Extra webhooks, API keys and status pages are switched off too.
- Everything comes back on if you subscribe again.

If you are on the old founders price of $4 a month, you lose it when you cancel. It cannot be restored.

## Downgrade

1. Open the **Plans** tab.
2. On the smaller plan you want, click **Downgrade to** that plan.
3. Read the summary of what changes, then confirm.

The change happens at the end of your current billing period. Until then, you keep your current plan. After that, anything above the new plan's limits is switched off, never deleted. It comes back if you upgrade again.

## If a payment fails

If we cannot charge your card, a red banner saying **Your last payment failed** appears across the app.

1. Click **Update card** in the banner.
2. Add a working card and set it as your default.

Your plan stays active while we try the charge again. If the payment still cannot be collected, your account moves to Free. Nothing is deleted. Monitors and other items above the Free limits are switched off and come back when you pay again.
